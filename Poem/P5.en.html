Journey of Memories

Home | Back

Night is a dark cup
that drips upon my soul.
The window is closed,
the stars are extinguished,
and only a trembling candle,
like a feverish heart,
beats in the darkness.

The wind comes—
it shakes,
it breaks,
it carries away.
A leaf falls,
a roaring wave cries,
and time, like an old page,
turns relentlessly.

Ah, yet—
through a narrow crack,
light shines!
The candle grows tall,
the leaf glows,
the wave calms,
and for a fleeting moment,
a breeze of hope brushes my thoughts,
like a bird in the cage of destiny imagining freedom.

But fate returns,
with heavy steps,
with wind again,
with wave again.
The candle bends,
the light sinks,
and hope in the darkness
is extinguished.

What remains?
But memory?
A memory that glows
not as a shadow,
but like a jewel in the heart's chest.

Candle.
Window.
Leaf.
Wave.
Page.

All remain within me.
All, like a hidden treasure.
And I learn:
Even in inevitable suffering,
the eternity of choices
shines in the light of memory.

Hasan Mousavi Takami
Paris
June 2025
