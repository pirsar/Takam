Life

The sky turns, like a pale lantern in the hand of the wind, its light scattering on the branches, on distant rivers, on the half-closed eyelids of sleep. The stars fall, not only to the soil, but upon the shoulders of birds, upon the silent waters of a lake, upon the cheek of a child resting on a mother’s lap. Each spark is a seed of life, taking root in the heart of darkness. The trees offer their branches to the night, like arms folded in a tender embrace. Leaves tremble, not from fear, but from a gentle breeze carrying the scent of rain and freshly turned earth. The earth breathes, slowly, and its breath settles on the grass, on petals of narcissus, on the damp skin of stones. The earth sings, its voice a lullaby whispered into the ears of every creature. And I, amidst this trembling light, see myself not as a lonely pillar, but as a stream among a thousand rivers. I mingle with life, with eyes, with hands, with voices, and I feel the world as an endless canvas, where every breath, every glance, every smile lays down a new color.

Hassan Mousavi Takami
Paris
June 2025
