Takom

I came to Takom — behold! a verdant, lovely land, And knew it was a work divine, a truth to understand. Its northern woods like tresses drape a lover’s gentle brow, And to the west, tall trees in pride and grace avow. Its southern face — a golden plain where fields entwine and dance, And in the east, the Tajen flows — a wine in meadow’s trance. How sweet the dawn — when roosters crow and sparrows trill in flight, They wake the hearts from slumber deep — with songs of pure delight. At dusk, the dogs and jackals cry, while lanterns gently glow, A golden war of sound and light — yet peace beneath does flow. For ages stands Takom upright — steadfast upon this crest, While Rika is a restless stream, still longing for its quest.
