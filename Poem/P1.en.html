Takom

I came to Takom, saw a lovely green paradise; I knew it was God's handiwork—so wise. To the north, the forest drapes like a lover's tress, And the west with its trees stands proud in full dress. The south is a golden plain, with dancing fields in tune, And in the east, the Tajan flows, like wine in a silver spoon. How sweet at dawn, the chirping of the jays, They wake the hearts—such songs of bonds and praise! The shepherds’ dogs and jackals cry and fight, Yet peace is caught in ropes of morning light. A thousand years Takom has stood, steadfast and grand, While Rika, the stream, dreams of distant land.
