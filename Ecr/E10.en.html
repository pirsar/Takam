The Secret of the Pasture

A Five-Act Play: Verza and Alba

Act I: The Meeting

The morning mist blankets the Hyrcanian forests of Mazandaran, Iran. Leaves glisten with raindrops, and the gentle sound of rain falls on the grass. Amidst the silence, Verza, a strong and patient cow of the forest, walks calmly.
Verza: I stand amidst mist and rain, speaking of ancient roots, and of humans who place law above the soil.
From afar, Alba emerges from the snowy pastures of the Alps; the cold mountain breeze, the sound of bells, and rushing streams accompany her.
Alba: I am Alba, from the snowy pastures, and I know that human reason sometimes loses the path of the heart.

Act II: Critique of Law and Reason

Ancient trees reach toward the sky, sunlight breaking through branches to fall on the grass. Verza steps gently on the soft soil.
Verza: They strike the soil and trees, yet their rigid laws and calculations forget the very ground beneath their hooves.
Alba stands atop a snowy hill, her gaze reaching the distant Alps.
Alba: In this neglect, peaks weep and rivers fall silent, for reason has forgotten that life is a flow, not just calculation.

Act III: The Wisdom of Nature

Grass waves under Verza’s feet, sunlight playing with droplets of dew.
Verza: I eat the grass, drink the rain, embrace the sun, without laws or accounts to confine me.
Alba walks beside a roaring stream, touching the waves with her nose.
Alba: I feel the snow, follow the river, and hear the silence of the Alps, where human reason can still be freed from calculation.

Act IV: Questions About the Future

Heavy clouds move over mountains and forests. Soft winds carry distant sounds.
Verza: Will this forest endure, or will it one day remain only a memory beneath rigid laws and calculating minds?
Alba: Will my peaks and rivers weep from the heat of the world? I fear a day when pastures fall silent, and humans remain only with pens and signatures, unaware of life itself.

Act V: Conclusion

The evening light falls upon grass and snow, and everything grows still. Silence and breeze whisper the secret of life.
Verza: The secret lies in this moist soil, which each year sprouts new grass without stamp or document. True law is life itself, not paper and calculation.
Alba: My secret lies in this small bell, guiding the pasture without a map, where reason can follow the heart’s path.
Verza & Alba (together): In simplicity and justice, life shines; in silence and heart’s wisdom, the voice of truth is heard.
